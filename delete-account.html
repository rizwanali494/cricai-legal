Delete your account

CricFellow by CricTeamUp – Find Cricket Teams

You can delete your CricFellow account and the data linked to it at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CricFellow and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Send an email from the email address you signed up with to support@example.com with the subject "Delete my CricFellow account". We'll confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile details and photo, and your Cricket Passport
  • Your posts, pending requests and videos
  • Speed Radar and LBW history
  • Your phone number, settings and saved location
  • Your team membership (and your team, if you are its only player)
  • Your login: you can't sign in to that account again

What is kept, without your name

Scorecards, match results, ratings and chats you took part in stay for the other players, with you shown as "Deleted player". This keeps their records and leaderboards correct. Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app you can delete individual posts, videos, Speed Radar and LBW history items, and clear optional passport fields such as your phone number. To ask us to delete other specific data, email support@example.com.

Before you delete

  • If you captain a team with other players, make one of them captain first.
  • If you are in a match that isn't finished, wait for the result or leave it first.
