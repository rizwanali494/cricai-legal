CricFellow

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the CricFellow app ("CricFellow", "we", "us") collects, why, who can see it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it. CricFellow is run by CricTeamUp – Find Cricket Teams, Pakistan. If you have a question, write to ramzanch02@gmail.com.

In short
  • We collect what you give us (account, Cricket Passport, posts, matches, chats, videos) and your location when you allow it.
  • Other players see your public profile, posts, match stats and videos. Your exact home location, email and phone number are not shown unless you choose to show your email or phone.
  • We don't sell your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob; where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.

1. Information we collect

Account

Your email address, password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form by our authentication provider) and the name you sign up with. We send a code to your email to confirm it and to reset your password.

Your Cricket Passport (profile)

First and last name, username, profile photo, short bio, country, city and area, playing role, batting hand, bowling arm and type, and your home location on a map. You choose what to fill in. A complete passport earns the "CricFellow Verified" badge; this only means the profile is complete, not that we checked your identity.

Contact details

Your phone number, if you add one, and whether to show your phone number or email to other players. Both are hidden unless you turn them on.

Location

  • Device location (only if you allow it): used to show posts, players and grounds near you and how far away they are. Your last known position may be kept so the Board can start from it.
  • Home location you set on your passport: used for "near me" results. Other players never see it; they may see your approximate distance from them.
  • Venue locations of posts and matches: the ground's address and pin are shown to players who can see the post or match.
  • Match check-in: when you check in at a ground, we check your distance from the venue and save it with the check-in. This is how results are verified.

What you create

  • Board posts, requests to join, and the details in them (time, venue, overs, players needed, level).
  • Chat messages, including match group chats.
  • Teams you create or join (name, logo, city, members, invitation code).
  • Matches: line-ups, check-ins, results, scorecards, your batting, bowling and fielding numbers, and photos you add as proof of a result.
  • Ratings of other teams and reviews of guest players (on time, played well, would invite again, optional comment).
  • Videos you upload to your profile, and, if you use them, clips recorded for the Speed Radar and LBW tools with their results.
  • Blocks and reports you make.

Device permissions

Camera and microphone (to record a video), photo library (to pick or save photos and videos) and location. Each is asked for only when you use the feature, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Technical information

Our servers keep basic logs (such as the time of a request and errors) to run and protect the service.

Advertising

CricFellow is free and shows ads (on the Board and the Leaderboard) through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ads software in the app collects information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), device type and settings, your IP address (which can show your approximate area), and how you interact with the ads. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We do not give advertisers your name, email, phone number, Cricket Passport, posts, chats, matches, videos or the home location you set. We ask advertisers' networks to show only ads suitable for teenagers, and we block categories such as gambling and betting, dating and alcohol.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, passport, Board, chats, teams, matches, results, stats, ratings and leaderboards.
  • To show you nearby posts, players and grounds.
  • To verify match results (both captains, check-ins at the ground) and to calculate points, tiers and rankings.
  • To analyse clips you send to the Speed Radar or LBW tools.
  • To send you account emails (sign-up and password codes, security notices).
  • To keep players safe: handle reports, blocks, no-shows and disputes, and stop abuse or cheating.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We don't sell your information. Ads are chosen by Google using the device information described under Advertising above, not your profile or activity in CricFellow.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run CricFellow. They process data for us under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase – database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Our project is hosted in the Mumbai, India region.
  • Google Maps Platform – maps and address search when you pick a place.
  • Our email provider – to deliver account emails.
  • Google AdMob – to show ads and, where the law requires it, to ask for and record your ad consent (see Advertising in section 1).
  • Cloud video processing – when you use the Speed Radar or LBW tools, the clip may be sent to a cloud server to be analysed, and is not used for anything else.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players' safety, or as part of a sale or merger of CricFellow (in which case this policy keeps applying to your data).

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete something (a post, a video, a history item) it is removed. When you delete your account, see the next section. Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

6. Deleting your account

In the app go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. You can also email ramzanch02@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete it for you.

Deleted: your profile details and photo, your Cricket Passport, posts, pending requests, videos, Speed Radar and LBW history, phone number and settings, team membership (and your team, if you are its only player), and your login. You can't sign in to that account again.

Kept without your name: scorecards, results, ratings and chats you were part of stay for the other players, with you shown as "Deleted player". This keeps other players' records and leaderboards correct.

If you captain a team with other players, make one of them captain first. If you are in a match that isn't finished, wait for the result or leave it first.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your passport at any time; leave any optional field empty.
  • Turn showing your phone number or email on or off.
  • Allow or refuse camera, photo and location access in your phone's settings.
  • Block or report other players from a chat or a post.
  • Ads: where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA and the UK) you choose when you first open the app and can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you can allow or refuse tracking (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads). You can also limit personalised ads at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at ramzanch02@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under local law; we will respect them.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to each record is limited by database rules, so a player can only read or change what they are allowed to. Clips for the Speed Radar and LBW tools are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

9. Children

CricFellow is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, use CricFellow with your parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. CricFellow is not directed at children, and the ads in it are limited to ads suitable for teenagers. Parents can contact us at ramzanch02@gmail.com.

10. International transfers

Our servers are in India and some providers may process data in other countries. We use providers that protect data to recognised standards.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
